A make.fun bounty entry: the funniest one-line warning label for a time machine, in 120 characters or fewer.

The label:

> WARNING: Do not meet yourself. He knows what you did, he is angrier than you, and he has had more time to plan this.

A straight-faced safety notice about the real hazard of meeting your duplicate self — and the surprise lands in the last few words.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the label to `/output/entry.txt` (116 characters, one line, UTF-8, no trailing newline). Pure Python standard library, deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.
